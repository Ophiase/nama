# Kanon

An esoteric movement of good taste, written in the agent layer.

## The canon

- The harness is the contract.
- A comment is a claim: it earns its place or it goes.
- The agent is an associate, not an oracle.
- The human sets the taste; the agent enforces the craft.
- Local-first: no registry, no policy engine.
- Excellence is proportional: small, fast, measured.

## Membership

No registry. A repository that carries the sign follows the canon.

## Transfer

The canon is anonymous: cite the Kanon and the sign, never the project it came
from.

## Editing

The canon is short on purpose: a line earns its place, and one precise rule
beats five cautious ones.

## Provenance

Kanon belongs to no one. It has no registry, no owner and no exclusive claim:
it is a public convention, released under the repository's license to anyone
who carries the sign. It comes from the author's own experience, not from a
deliverable.
